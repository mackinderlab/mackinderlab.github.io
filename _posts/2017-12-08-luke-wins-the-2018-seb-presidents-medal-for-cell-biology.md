---
title: Luke wins the 2018 SEB President's Medal for Cell Biology
date: 2017-12-08
---

Luke has been awarded the 2018 Society for Experimental Biology (SEB) [President's medal for Cell Biology](https://www.sebiology.org/events/event-sub-pages/seb-florence-2018/awards-and-prizes/president-medallists#luke). The SEB President’s Medals are awarded annually to young scientists of outstanding merit. This year’s awards will be presented at the Annual Meeting, SEB Florence 2018. "It was unexpected to receive this accolade at such an early stage and I would like to recognise the hard work of all those on my team, my collaborators and thank those who have taught and mentored me over the years".
