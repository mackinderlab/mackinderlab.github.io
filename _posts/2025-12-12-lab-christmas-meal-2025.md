To celebrate the end of 2025, the lab took a break from the bench and headed out for a mini golf social at the Hole in Wand. It was a fun way to unwind together, with plenty of laughs, friendly competition, and a few unexpectedly impressive shots along the way.

Afterwards, we continued the celebrations over dinner at Giggling Squid, sharing great food and even better conversation. All in all, it was a great evening, and a perfect way to wrap up the year as a team.
