## James speaks at the SYNMIKRO symposium in Marburg

James travelled to Marburg, Germany, for the 13th annual SYNMIKRO symposium, *Green by Design: Understanding and Engineering the Powerhouse of Photosynthesis*. He gave a talk on the **convergent evolution of CO₂-fixing biomolecular condensates**, alongside speakers working on synthetic chloroplast genomes, diatom photosynthesis and engineered carbon fixation.

The talks were held on the main screen of Marburg's city cinema, which made for a memorable venue. There was also a traditional German dinner, with plenty of Schnitzel.

It was also great to catch up with Mel Ludwig and Laura Dorner, who have both visited the lab before. Many thanks to Felix Willmund and the SYNMIKRO organisers for the invitation.
