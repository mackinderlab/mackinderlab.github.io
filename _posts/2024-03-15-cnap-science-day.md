title: CNAP Science Day
date: 2024-03-15

Every year the Centre of Novel Agricultural Products gathers together to share the science and projects which were worked on last year and celebrate the people who make it possible.

This year too Mackinder lab attended, with the talks by James and Onyou adding to the excellent programme of speakers.

As usual, we had a great time learning more about the incredible science and networking with other researchers at York.
