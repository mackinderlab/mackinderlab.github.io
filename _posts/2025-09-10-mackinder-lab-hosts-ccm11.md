The Mackinder Lab was delighted to host CCM11 at the University of York. Thank you to all who presented. The standard of the talks and posters was outstanding.

Thank you to Plant Physiology, Agrisera, ISPR, WALZ, and TWIST for their generous funding and sponsorship of the event. Without this support, the fully funded meeting would not have been possible.

We are looking forward to the next meeting hosted by Yusuke Matsuda from Kwansei Gakuin University. See you all in CCM12 in 2028!
