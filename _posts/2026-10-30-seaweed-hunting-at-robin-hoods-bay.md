James, Herbie and Kate swapped the lab for the North Yorkshire coast this week, heading to Robin Hood’s Bay to collect seaweeds. A few days of storms had left the beach covered in washed-up macroalgae: a green tide of *Ulva* (the star of our [recent preprint](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.07.16.738900v1.full)) and huge piles of kelp. Herbie’s keen eye spotted our main target first, blades of Bangiales, the red algal group that includes *Porphyra*. We’ll be using them to find out how red algae organise their Rubisco. Kate was on the hunt for Ectocarpales, and along the way we turned up Irish moss (*Chondrus crispus*), *Laminaria* and plenty of other great seaweeds. Everything is now back in the lab, and the Bangiales are off for DNA barcoding to pin down exactly which species we found.
