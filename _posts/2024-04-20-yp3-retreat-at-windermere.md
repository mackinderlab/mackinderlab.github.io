---
title: YP3 retreat at Windermere
date: 2024-04-20
---

Collaboration and communication is what makes science great, and it lays at heart of the Mackinder lab. This spring the York Physics of Pyrenoids Project (YP3) team organised a knowledge exchange retreat. We met with other groups researching the pyrenoids, LLPS, and carbon fixation to discuss the latest results in the field and share ideas with the beautiful backdrop of the English Lake District.
