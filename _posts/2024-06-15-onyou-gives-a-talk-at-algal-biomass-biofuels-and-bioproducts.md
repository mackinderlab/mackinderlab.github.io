---
title: Onyou gives a talk at Algal Biomass, Biofuels and Bioproducts (AlgalBBB 2024)
date: 2024-06-15
---

Every year, the algal biotech research community meets to discuss the latest research and technologies. This year, the conference took place in Clearwater, Florida. Onyou presented the tools we developed to localise proteins in the diatom and shared their potential for applications in the field.
