For this year’s Christmas Social we decided to unleash our creativity at a pottery painting workshop. For a few hours we exchanged pipettes and test tubes for brushes and sponges and had much fun creating little works of art on mugs and plates. The topics were diverse and ranged from abstract patterns, cartoon characters to realistic depictions of nature (including algae and seaweed too!). We then concluded our day with a delicious meal at Marzano’s.
