Living in York

York is continually ranked as one of the best places to live in the UK. It is steeped in history with Roman origins, has lots going on with great museums, cafes and restaurants, has excellent connections to the rest of the UK and is surrounded by beautiful countryside. Everywhere, including the University, is within walking or biking distance from the city centre.

​Opportunities

​We are a growing lab looking to recruit highly motivated and exceptional individuals who want to be part of a fun and exciting team doing cutting edge systems and synthetic biology in algae. 
Postgraduate and postdoc opportunities: I am seeking exceptionally motivated and creative individuals who wish to join the team. I am able to support research fellows by supplying lab space and covering the majority of laboratory costs.
