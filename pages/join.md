We are looking for highly motivated and exceptional people who want to be part
of a fun and exciting team doing cutting-edge systems and synthetic biology in
algae.

To apply, email [Luke](mailto:luke.mackinder@york.ac.uk) with a cover letter
describing your research interests, your CV, and contact details for three
referees.

## Postdocs and research fellows

Luke is happy to support applications for independent fellowships, providing
lab space and covering most lab costs. Schemes to consider include:

- [EMBO Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://www.embo.org/funding/fellowships-grants-and-career-support/postdoctoral-fellowships/)
- [Human Frontier Science Program](https://www.hfsp.org/funding/hfsp-funding/postdoctoral-fellowships)
- [Life Sciences Research Foundation](https://lsrf.org/)
- [Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/)
- [DFG Walter Benjamin Programme](https://www.dfg.de/en/research_funding/programmes/individual/walter_benjamin/index.html)

## University of York BSc and MSc projects

If you are a York student with an idea for an independent project in our area,
please get in touch.

## Living in York

York is one of the most desirable places to live in the UK, with its Roman
and medieval heritage, museums, restaurants, excellent rail links and
beautiful countryside close by. The university and city centre are within
easy walking or cycling distance.
