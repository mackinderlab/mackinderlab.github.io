**Data and methods for high-throughput protein tagging in *Synechococcus elongatus* PCC 7942.**

CyanoTag uses high-throughput fluorescent tagging to discover the functions of
proteins in a model photosynthetic bacterium. The project has targeted over 700
of the 2,714 proteins in *S. elongatus* (figures as of mid 2024).

The work is published in *Science Advances*:
Perrin AJ, Dowson M, Davis K, Nam O, Dowle AA, Calder G, Springthorpe VJ, Zhao G, Mackinder LCM (2025).
[CyanoTag: Discovery of protein function facilitated by high-throughput endogenous tagging in a photosynthetic prokaryote](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adp6599).
