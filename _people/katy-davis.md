Katy completed her PhD in Biochemistry at the University of Cambridge, focussing on Golgi-localised enzymes involved in plant cell wall biosynthesis. She has now returned to York (where she completed her undergraduate degree in biochemistry) to join the Mackinder lab, and is investigating proteins involved in membrane remodelling in Chlamydomonas. In her spare time, Katy enjoys making artwork and experimenting with different media (with varying rates of success!).
