In 2023 I graduated from the University of York with a degree in Biology with a year in industry. During my degree I completed a Generation Research summer studentship with Dr Charlotte Walker and Professor Luke Mackinder investigating inorganic carbon uptake in the model alga *Chlamydomonas reinhardtii*. This was where I first developed my interest in carbon concentrating mechanisms, the pyrenoid, and of membrane-less organelles broadly. After a brief stint as a research technician at the University of Sheffield, I have come back to the Mackinder lab to do a PhD investigating the unique structural proteins surrounding the pyrenoid of the diatom *Thalassiosira pseudonana*. We hope to investigate their function in the carbon concentrating mechanism of this important organism through cryo electron microscopy and in vivo manipulation. Outside the lab I am an avid rock climber.
