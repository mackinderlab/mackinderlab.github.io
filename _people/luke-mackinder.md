Luke completed his undergraduate degree in Natural Sciences at Durham University, UK, graduating in Organic Chemistry and Molecular Biology. His research has ranged from viral infection of marine phytoplankton to the algal CO2-concentrating mechanism, which he studied as a postdoc at the Carnegie Institution for Science at Stanford. He joined the University of York as a Lecturer in October 2016.
